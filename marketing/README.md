# DigitalBurj launch kit

Source: the public copy and supplied assets in `dist/` on the repository's default branch, reviewed 29 September 2026. This is a publishing kit, not evidence that any social account or campaign has been launched.

## Positioning

**Brand promise:** Learn. Build. Transform.

**Short description:** DigitalBurj helps growing businesses organize operations, apply AI with human oversight, and build software around real workflows. Its Academy develops practical skills through tasks, feedback and evidence of work. Its Studio builds tailored web and mobile products.

**Editorial order:** Business first, Academy second, Studio third. Growth, industry solutions and talent support specific stories when there is a useful example. Do not imply every customer shares one account across these services until the product and account architecture supports that experience.

| Audience | Problem to lead with | Offer to explain | Next step |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| SME owner or operations lead | Work scattered across spreadsheets, chat and separate systems | Business OS, AI and workflow review | `/get-started` |
| Career changer or practical learner | Learning without proof of doing | Academy tasks, practice and review | `/academy` |
| Team with a unique workflow | Off-the-shelf software does not fit | Studio discovery and tailored build | `/studio` |

## Voice and visual rules

- Speak as a practitioner: show a workflow, decision, screen, task or result before naming technology.
- Use the site's charcoal `#0f1714`, warm off-white `#f6f5f1`, orange `#f23a1d`, and lighter orange `#ff7a45`. Use Instrument Sans and Plus Jakarta Sans where licensed and available; use system sans as fallback.
- Use the actual brand assets in `dist/brand/`. Preserve proportions and clear space; never redraw the logo from text or place the colored mark on a low-contrast image.
- Photography should show real people, work, places and product screens with permission. Label concept mockups and simulations. No invented customer logos, testimonials, ROI numbers, certifications or operational status.
- Keep a single action per post. Link to the relevant page with tagged links, e.g. `https://www.digitalburj.com/get-started?utm_source=linkedin&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=launch_2026q4&utm_content=business_workflow_01`.

## Account setup checklist

Reserve a consistent `DigitalBurj` handle on LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook, YouTube and TikTok; check availability before printing or announcing it. Use the repository wordmark as profile branding where it remains legible, and the mark as the avatar. Set website link, business category, contact destination, UAE location if accurate, and account recovery with two admins plus MFA. Connect a shared business inbox and record account owner, access and recovery method privately.

**LinkedIn description:** DigitalBurj helps SMEs turn scattered operations into clearer workflows and useful software. Explore Business OS and AI, practical skills through Academy, and tailored products from Studio. Learn. Build. Transform.

**Instagram / TikTok bio:** Better workflows. Real skills. Useful software. Business · Academy · Studio ↓

**YouTube description:** Practical breakdowns of SME operations, AI with oversight, software builds and work-based learning from DigitalBurj. Explore the website for services and Academy updates.

**Pinned profile links:** Business `/get-started`; Academy `/academy`; Studio `/studio`. Keep one primary bio link to the website if the platform offers only one.

## Launch sequence

1. **Days 1–3:** Claim accounts, apply brand assets, verify links, publish the three pinned posts below. Do a mobile legibility check on each platform.
2. **Days 4–14:** Publish three feed posts and two short videos weekly. Answer relevant comments and messages each workday. Log actual questions without copying private customer data.
3. **Days 15–30:** Publish one real workflow walkthrough, one Academy task demonstration, and one Studio build decision each week. Request permission for any identifiable client example. Review analytics weekly and double down on qualified conversations, not views alone.

| Week | Mon | Wed | Fri | Short video / story |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Brand introduction | Business workflow | Academy method | Studio build process; founder introduction |
| 2 | Lost enquiry breakdown | Approval before automation | A learner task and rubric | 30-second workflow demo; FAQ |
| 3 | Data handoff diagram | Academy critique process | Studio discovery questions | Screen walkthrough; founder lesson |
| 4 | Owner visibility example | What a useful portfolio proves | When to build custom software | FAQ response; month recap |

Use real screen recordings when available. Blur names, phone numbers, financial figures and account data. When a real demonstration is not available, use a clearly labeled conceptual diagram.

## First three pinned posts — publish-ready copy

### 1. DigitalBurj / introduction

**Visual:** `post-template.html` with a short brand statement, or the supplied Open Graph art.

**LinkedIn / Instagram:**

A business can have plenty of tools and still lose track of the work.

An enquiry arrives in chat. The handoff lives in a spreadsheet. An approval waits in someone’s inbox. The owner only sees the gap after it becomes a problem.

DigitalBurj works across three practical areas:

• Business: clearer workflows, software and AI with oversight.  
• Academy: skills built through tasks, feedback and proof of work.  
• Studio: tailored products for workflows that need a custom build.

We’ll share the decisions, screens and lessons behind that work here. Start with the problem you want to solve: https://www.digitalburj.com/get-started

#DigitalBurj #BusinessOperations #DigitalTransformation

### 2. Business / enquiry workflow

**Visual:** five-frame carousel: enquiry → owner → next action → approval → visible outcome. Label it “Example workflow”.

**Caption:**

A lead should not depend on someone remembering to forward a message.

Map the path: where does the enquiry arrive, who owns it, what happens next, when is approval needed, and how does the owner know it was resolved?

That map is the starting point for a useful CRM or automation. Tell us where your handoff breaks: https://www.digitalburj.com/get-started

#SMEOperations #CRM #WorkflowDesign

### 3. Academy / learning by doing

**Visual:** task card → submitted artifact → feedback → revised artifact. Label as a sample task if no real cohort exists.

**Caption:**

Watching a lesson is a beginning. Applying it to a brief, explaining the decision and improving the work is where skill becomes visible.

DigitalBurj Academy is built around practical tasks and review. We’ll show sample briefs, common mistakes and what stronger work looks like. Explore the Academy: https://www.digitalburj.com/academy

#PracticalLearning #DigitalSkills #DigitalBurjAcademy

## Three short video scripts

**Business, 25–35 seconds:** Show a consented or simulated enquiry crossing chat, spreadsheet and an inbox. Voice: “One enquiry. Three places to lose it. First map who owns it, what happens next, and where approval belongs. Then choose the software. That is how we approach business workflows at DigitalBurj.” End card: “Start with a problem / digitalburj.com/get-started.” On screen label simulated interfaces.

**Academy, 25–35 seconds:** Show a sample brief, the learner’s first attempt, two concrete reviewer notes and a revised result. Voice: “A certificate alone cannot show how someone works. A task can. Here is the brief, the first attempt, the feedback and the revision.” End card: “Explore Academy / digitalburj.com/academy.”

**Studio, 25–35 seconds:** Show a workflow sketch, a decision about off-the-shelf versus custom, and a labeled prototype screen. Voice: “Custom software starts with an expensive question: which part is actually unique? We map the workflow first, then build only what needs to be specific to the team.” End card: “Explore Studio / digitalburj.com/studio.”

## Weekly measurement

Track date, channel, format, topic, landing page, reach, saves, qualified messages, booked discovery calls, Academy enquiries and Studio enquiries. Record UTM source/medium/campaign/content for each link. Review the conversion from qualified conversation to booking; do not optimize solely for follower count. At day 30, retain the two topics producing the best qualified conversations, revise weak hooks, and document the questions that deserve a product page or demonstration.

## Publishing gate

Before publishing: verify each offer and URL against the live site; confirm any “live”, “free”, “certified”, integration or price claim with the responsible owner; get consent for identifiable footage, screenshots and testimonials; check mobile crop, captions, alt text and links.
